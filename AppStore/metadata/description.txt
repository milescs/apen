Apen turns your voice into text in any app, without sending a single word to the cloud.

Press Option-Space (or your own shortcut), talk, and press it again. Apen transcribes on your Mac's Neural Engine and pastes the text wherever your cursor is: a prompt box, a code editor, an email, a chat.

DICTATE ANYWHERE
• Works in every app: AI chat apps, code editors, terminals, mail and notes
• Tap the shortcut to start and stop, or hold it while you talk
• See the transcript while you speak; the final text is ready about a second after you stop, even after ten minutes
• Switch microphones right from the menu bar

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Speech recognition runs on your Mac with NVIDIA's open-weight Parakeet model
• Nothing you say leaves your Mac: no account, no analytics, no servers
• Models load only while you dictate and are released right after

OPTIONAL CLEANUP WITH A LOCAL LLM
• Removes filler words and false starts, fixes punctuation
• Modes for coding prompts, writing, messages, email and notes, chosen automatically by the app you're typing in
• Your dictation is tidied, never answered or rewritten into something else

YOUR WORDS, YOUR DICTIONARY
• Teach Apen your acronyms and jargon: say "RLS", get "row-level security"
• Fix names the speech model tends to get wrong

HISTORY AND FILES
• Every transcript is saved on your Mac with one-click copy
• Search everything you've dictated
• Transcribe audio and video files too

Apen is free and open source: github.com/milescs/apen

Requires a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later. The speech model (about 600 MB) downloads once when you first set up Apen; the optional cleanup model is 2.5 GB.
