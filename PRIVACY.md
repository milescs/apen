# Apen privacy policy

_Last updated: September 29, 2026_

Apen is a dictation app that runs entirely on your Mac.

**What Apen does not do**

- It does not collect, store or send your voice, transcripts, or any other personal data to its developer or to
  any third party.
- It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting.

**What stays on your Mac**

- Audio is processed on your Mac and is not kept unless you turn on "Keep audio recordings" in Settings. The one
  exception: if a transcription fails, its recording is kept so you can retry it.
- Your transcript history and personal dictionary are saved in a database on your Mac. You can delete entries or
  the whole history at any time.
- The optional cleanup model runs on your Mac as well.

**Network access**

Apen connects to the internet only when you ask it to download a model. The speech models and the optional
cleanup model are downloaded from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). As with any download, Hugging Face can see your IP
address and the files requested, under its own privacy policy. No transcripts or audio are ever uploaded.

**Permissions**

- **Microphone**: used only while you are dictating.
- **Accessibility**: used only to press ⌘V once so the text you just dictated is pasted into the field you're typing
  in. Without it, Apen copies the text to the clipboard instead.

**Contact**

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/milescs/apen/issues.
